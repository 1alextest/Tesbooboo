## 3. Findings raised and their actual dispositions

### QA-ALLOC-R01 — preserving a legacy shape cannot exempt new authoring from current policy

- **Severity/category:** Major / Boundary and Spec-Code-Drift.
- **Finding:** Candidate §§4–5 retain existing and continuing `stage_testcase_changes`, chat and sweep producers as `legacy_generation_review`, while new governed proposals get immutable submissions, independent decisions and logical commitments. Read literally without a cutover contract, a caller could choose a legacy entrance for newly governed work and obtain weaker current behavior. Historical preservation and source compatibility do not themselves authorize a permanent alternate promotion route.
- **Evidence:** Current `stageTestcaseChanges` inserts `provider='external_proposal'` into the shared root; `zyraSaveAttempt` uses current snapshot-bound selected indexes, while its narrower current contract does not supply every FR-027–037/R-ASE-34 guarantee. The actual preview is default-off. v1.4.34 requires all-writer convergence and truthful unknown historical guidance; it does not ratify an indefinite route-based exception.
- **Impact:** New content could bypass required effective guidance, immutable submission or current exact review through its choice of producer/old Save alias, even with no wrong-kind cross-write.
- **Recommendation:** Treat kind as representation; choose the policy cutover at affected project/capability and authoritative writers. Keep original history readable; require explicit fenced adoption and current exact review where a pending legacy batch cannot satisfy the governed contract. No invented prior Skill consumption or operation ancestry.
- **Author response:** Current author §8 explicitly adopts that rule. It names generator, chat/regeneration, sweep, external staging, model-generated proposals and all protected promotion/Save aliases; puts shared applicability/effective-guidance checks before enablement; preserves original snapshots; fences adopted old mutation routes; blocks only affected unavailable capabilities; and retains legitimate human Draft and raw-observation paths.
- **Status:** **Addressed in the reviewed design. Implementation/enablement OPEN.** This does not authorize turning on the preview or certify all existing producers.
- **Discriminating proof:** After governed cutover, present equivalent new content through new staging, old external staging, chat regeneration and old Save. No route obtains weaker required protection. A pre-cutover immutable historical receipt remains readable; a properly adopted/guided submission is usable. Missing historical guidance must not be “backfilled” as consumed.

### QA-ALLOC-R02 — exact proposal/native hashes do not detect new contradictory evidence

- **Severity/category:** Major / Invariant and Failure.
- **Finding:** A human may approve operation O based on original report R1; linked retest R2 may finalize before apply without changing O’s payload or native base. A guard that checks only those hashes would apply the still-exact old decision despite new material evidence. Requiring a later analyst to voluntarily edit O leaves a race and a missing consequence owner.
- **Derivation:** Frozen R-QA-P04/P06 tested stable decision meaning and distinct retest evidence; v1.4.34 FR-009 expressly requires new material evidence to reopen affected proposals/renew approval. This check was raised after reviewing the candidate and before the author’s §9 remedy.
- **Impact:** Evidence contradicting a defect, expected result or accepted observation could arrive through the proper report owner but fail to block stale publication.
- **Recommendation:** Bind explicit relevant task/report/finding context revisions to submissions/decisions at the existing proposal owner, advance relevant context at finalization, and recheck it under apply locks. Relevant new evidence requires review; an authorized reasoned unaffected disposition may establish current context. Do not invalidate unrelated project proposals or automatically declare the old observation false.
- **Author response:** Current §9 supplies the context-revision owner, explicit same-project causal links, invalidation without payload change, exact apply recheck, human relevance disposition, historical commitment preservation and unrelated-report control.
- **Status:** **Addressed in the reviewed design. Implementation/enablement OPEN.** The report/apply race must use a concrete consistent locking or revision-CAS protocol so the check cannot race finalization. Merely sending a best-effort invalidation notification is insufficient.
- **Discriminating proof:** Approve O; finalize linked R2; leave O/native hashes unchanged; apply must refuse with no selected native effects. Repeat with unrelated R3; O remains eligible. Race finalize against apply: the serialized winner determines whether a commitment precedes new evidence or stale approval is refused. Preserve both originals and any prior valid commitment.

These are design findings and remedies, not reproduced production exploits. Neither finding requires a different task root once the consequence is placed at the correct existing owner.

## 5. Alternatives and compression result

**Reuse unchanged is falsified** for external task creation, immutable original custody and generalized review: current `aiGenerate` calls provider generation and requires a key; current Run/Automation result rows require accepted case context; mutable generation payload and size-only verification cannot meet the report contract.

**Extension A is viable** because its explicit type boundary can preserve the native identity/sequence/FK and current positive contracts while assigning genuinely new cardinalities to owned child facts. Its justification is locality and compatibility, not the name of the table. A requires meaningful work in the existing consumers; no claim of low effort follows from reusing a root.

**Extraction B remains a legitimate alternative.** One common root with the same existing IDs and typed dependents can satisfy these invariants. It would need source-to-root ownership/sequence/FK migration and still require custody, grants, reports, logical operations and native transaction adapters. This review found no invariant that makes that extra move necessary now. If implementation shows A’s conditional shapes or consumer boundaries cannot be enforced coherently, B should be reconsidered without pretending the earlier recommendation was a product requirement.

**Composition remains necessary under either choice.** Cases/history, accepted-case Runs, Bugs, KB, actors/credentials, storage and audit retain their native owners. A physically separate table under the same logical domain is not automatically a second product; ownership and invariant coherence decide that question. Conversely, several unrelated lifecycles sharing one table do not automatically establish reuse. This distinction preserves valid alternatives while rejecting an independently writable second case/proposal/review truth.

**Projection-only works for** board columns, queues, summaries, remaining-work/terminal labels and cross-module navigation. It fails for consumed prompt, assigned authority, actual receipt and commitment because those facts cannot be reconstructed from current labels or derived counters. Prompt and analysis can share a typed immutable-content helper, while report pre-finalization custody requires its own lifecycle. No new runner, general workflow engine, vendor registry or report paragraph store is justified.
