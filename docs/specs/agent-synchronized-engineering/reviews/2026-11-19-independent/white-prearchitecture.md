### Consequences and mutation after reliance

The evidence supports the following binding semantic boundaries; it does not settle their physical implementation:

- Upload reservations, quota use, claim leases and retention/holds are finite obligations with ownership for expiry, release and recovery. Default synthetic-data caps are not measured production capacity or an SLA. Source capture, impact work and delivery retries need explicit recovery ownership; notification delivery is not obligation completion.
