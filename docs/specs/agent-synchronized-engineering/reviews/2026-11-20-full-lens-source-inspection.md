## Material findings and actionable disposition

### F-01 — Canonical-version evidence and current claim mismatch (HIGH; verified provenance gap)

The six subordinate views reference canonical v1.4.29, while Library records are v1.4.18. Current subordinate README asserts recovered v1.4.28 ZIP with exact digest; PR narrative and the older `2026-11-19-gex-integration-gate.md` retain earlier byte-blocker language. The recovered ZIP claim may be correct, but this session did not verify its original bytes or the complete new canonical 1.4.29 artifact.

**Risk:** Reviewers may claim complete coverage/freeze on a different revision, or dismiss an actual recovered archive as unavailable.

**Required proof:** Identify and integrity-check the eight exact v1.4.28 originals and the proposed 1.4.29 derivative/manifest; reconcile the earlier blocker with dated evidence; pin all inputs for reviewers and forbid cross-revision “PASS” inheritance. Preserve historical notes as historical rather than rewriting them.

**Positive:** Old revision remains readable with correct old status; new revision cannot silently supersede it. **Negative:** Mix six new subordinate views with eight 1.4.18 originals and expect canonical assurance to reject “fully reviewed.”

### F-02 — New semantic graph outcomes lack a refreshed full-journey coverage inventory (HIGH; verified scope gap)

The 49 GJ-01..49 ledger was independently reconstructed for an earlier integration scope; the current implementation plan says earlier Grey findings remain historical. Proposed GEX-SEM-01..08, GEX-DISC-01..05 and ASE-FLOW-17..54 extend requirements/engineering checks but are **not** a new independently reconstructed full journey inventory.

**Risk:** A pass over the historical 49 journeys could incorrectly be presented as full assurance of policy/manual semantic reasoning, inferred relationships, disputed authority, correction propagation and user-facing impact handling.

**Required proof:** Fresh four-source FJCC inventory and accountable per-journey applicability for existing 49 plus new materially distinct user goals; independently verify positive/negative/forbidden/recovery and permissions; allow unchanged/N/A. Do not inflate journeys by copying acceptance-case labels.

### F-03 — Derived semantic correction needs an all-consumer invalidation oracle (HIGH; contract verification gap)

Requirements GEX semantic discovery DISC-05 and Design §14 specify append-only correction of inferred meanings and re-evaluation of dependent candidate impacts. ASER-C06 governs list/search/RAG/notification/export/cache readers generically. The current text does not establish a concrete **end-to-end discriminating read consistency** contract across a graph explanation, change-summary, search result, release projection and previously delivered notification when the underlying inferred claim is rejected or its effective source changes.

**Risk:** One view marks the hypothesis rejected while another continues presenting its old effect as current release support.

**Required proof:** A correction receipt and source-version fence should make every in-scope derived consumer either show fresh corrected state, explicitly stale/partial or block relied-on publication, with bounded regeneration and authorization. No historical run/result mutation. Existing owner implementation may already provide primitives; reuse before adding schema.

### F-04 — Human-facing temporal authority joins need exact source/build oracles (MEDIUM–HIGH; specification precision)

R-ASE-08/09/19 and semantic refinement specify effectivity, publication, “as-of” and target build differences. ASER-C02 pins revisions and validity. The contract would benefit from a single discriminating oracle for a manual accurately describing an *old* policy, a policy effective before its code was deployed, and a historical test conducted against an older build.

**Risk:** Meaning and conformance can be conflated across valid time, publication time, observation time and deployment time despite each record individually having a revision.

**Required proof:** Test a named historical as-of request and a current request over conflicting effective/publication/deployment intervals, including absent deployment identity. Assess whether native existing fields already support the join. This amplifies proposed GEX-DISC-03, not a newly unanticipated model.

### F-06 — Inference can reveal restricted information without exposing raw edges (HIGH security hypothesis, not exploit)

Authorized policy owner retains useful detail. Reject rather than infer from inaccessible mandatory evidence. Preserve practical budget and truthful UNKNOWN.
