## 4. Independent problem model and semantic completeness

A tester's useful result is a defensible answer to **what should be checked, why it matters, how to perform it, and what the actual evidence supports**. The necessary chain starts with the person's job and authorized expectations; a graph is one representation of the relationships that help answer those questions. It must also work when no source graph or formal model exists.

Material actors are an authorized human tester, requirement/policy decision owner, analyst client, separately authorized executor, reviewer/publisher, guidance-governance human and downstream decision consumer. One person may fill multiple jobs, but that does not merge authority or evidence.

The material truths are accepted expectations, current observations, proposed interpretations, official definitions, exact review decisions, applicability selections, attempted actions and results, original evidence, relationship meaning, source freshness, and method provenance. They need separate ownership because each can legitimately disagree with the others.

### Bounded abstraction/transfer check

The unchanged reasoning obligation used here was: **identify the truth and accountable owner before treating a derived or observed value as authority, and preserve the evidence boundary when it cannot be established.**

- In a hypothetical offline inventory audit, a scanner's counted stock is an observation, not approval to change a contractual reorder threshold. A discrepancy can be recorded without inventing a governance workflow for every scan.
- In a hypothetical scientific file reprocessing tool, a reproducible transformation may be the intended output, without any human policy approval requirement. The valid alternative is versioned deterministic configuration and retained inputs; adding universal human approval would overgeneralize the rule.
- With a missing calibration/source record, the same tool may retain the observation while marking the claimed accuracy UNKNOWN. A missing record does not prove a particular fault.

These are G1 conceptual controls and counterexamples, selected after reading the candidate. They support the abstraction discussion only. They are not execution evidence, independent discovery, a complete orchestration transfer receipt or proof that canonical 06 improves reviewer performance.
