## Admission and next decision

The exercise supports only successful bounded application to these two synthetic briefs. It does not demonstrate better opportunity discovery, fewer real defects, general transfer, human usefulness or that the earlier graph direction would have been discovered. The originating discovery regression was not independently replayed. One pair of planners on two tasks, the shared model family and the limited incumbent excerpt prevent broader causal claims.

Retain the candidate as proposed application guidance under the existing method owner; do not install, approve, activate or call it validated for improved discovery. Existing principle coverage may already be adequate, with application and evidence recording needing improvement. An unchanged method, rejected amendment or narrower wording remains a valid result. Any broader promotion still needs the original regression and the unchanged charter's discriminating transfer/admission evidence, independent authority, monitoring and rollback.
