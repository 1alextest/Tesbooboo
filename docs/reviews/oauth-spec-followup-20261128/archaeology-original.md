## What can be said about “why”

The original review was scoped to a draft and pre-OAuth source, and explicitly ended with implementation and external evidence unresolved. It recovered the right high-level obligations but left their concrete deployment/session realization and closure for later work. The later implementation added material choices that require reassessment: login split between origins, OAuth allowlist default, provider-dependent network requests, and seam-specific fixtures. The retained later review demonstrates these still-open joins and policy questions. It does not establish that the earlier analyst ignored a known rule, that all later teams reviewed the actual topology, that any release gate knowingly waived them, or why a person made a choice.

The original accumulated record is also heterogeneous: one-analyst lens review (`S assurance:3,81`), four later external research passes unable to access the private diff (`:87`), then seven separately created 28 November agents with shared historical context (`T review README:19–31`). These are different review scopes. The November 28 outcomes were informed by prior observations and supplied candidates; they cannot retrospectively prove that a revised generic method would have independently discovered the old gaps.
